Lines and chunks

In IronGarden you review a pull request change by change, not file by file. Every changed line is either accepted by you, accepted by a rule, or still waiting.

Changes and chunks

Section titled “Changes and chunks”

A change is one added or removed line, or a removed line and the added line that replaced it, which count as one. A chunk is a range of adjacent lines in a file.

Every count in the review counts changes, not lines: “3 changes, none accepted”, “1 of 3 changes accepted”. A change is the smallest thing you can accept. How to accept them is in Accepting changes.

How a diff is shown

Section titled “How a diff is shown”
  • Unified is the default. Each row has two line-number columns, the old file’s then the new file’s, a + or −, then the code in syntax colours. Removed lines are red and added lines green.
  • When an added line replaces a removed one, the characters that differ between the two are marked.
  • Split shows Before on the left and After on the right. Each chunk (in Files) and each range card (in Brackets) has its own Unified/Split switch.
  • Unchanged lines between chunks fold into a stripe such as “42 unchanged lines”. Its arrow buttons show 20 more lines from the top or the bottom. When the fold hides the start of the function or type the next chunk sits in, that declaration’s first line appears on the stripe as a button that opens the fold up to it.
  • Left of the line numbers sit three narrow columns: the box that accepts a run of changed lines, each change’s bar, and the note column for comments.

Added, deleted and renamed files

Section titled “Added, deleted and renamed files”
  • Added file: every line is added, numbered on the new side only.
  • Deleted file: every line is removed. Ranges and comments on it count the old file’s lines.
  • Renamed file: IronGarden does not detect renames. A renamed file shows as its old path deleted and its new path added. When the agent marks the new lines as moved from the old path, the move’s bracket compares them with the original, and an unchanged rename reads as “Moved as is”. See Chunk context.
  • Binary or too large: no lines are shown (“Binary file. Its diff is not shown.” or “Too large to show (…)”). The whole file counts as one change, accepted with the file’s Accept. Changed images show their two versions; see Files view.
  • Generated files: a file the agent put in a bracket of kind generated waits behind “Generated file. Its diff is hidden.” until you click Load diff.

How lines are numbered

Section titled “How lines are numbered”

Brackets, comments and the chat all name lines the same way: by their number in the new version of the file. A removed line belongs to the new line that follows it, or to the last line when it ends the file. In a deleted file, numbers count the old version.

Commenting on a line range

Section titled “Commenting on a line range”
  1. Point at a line in Files or Brackets. A + button appears in the note column, labelled “Comment on line N”.
  2. Click it for that line alone. To take in more lines, drag down from it, or shift-click the + of another line. A New comment card opens under the last line, showing the range, such as “lines 17–18”.
  3. Write the comment. Markdown works.
  4. Pick what it is with the arrow beside the submit button (Kind of comment): Accept, Flag, Comment or Ask. The button takes that name.
  5. Click it.

A range with comments shows a mark in the note column on its first line: a speech bubble, or a flag while a flag on it is open, with a count when several threads share the lines. Threads whose lines overlap share one card under their last line. Close a card with its ×; click the mark to open it again.

Replies are one level deep: Reply answers the comment that starts the thread, never a reply. A reply goes out at once, and only to a comment that has already gone out.

Agents comment too, marked with an agent tag, on an unorthodox decision or hard code, and they answer your comments. Agents cannot flag, accept or ask.

Comment, flag, accept or ask

Section titled “Comment, flag, accept or ask”
KindWhat it doesWhen it goes out
CommentA note on the linesWith your review
FlagAsks for a change. It stays open until the person who set it resolves itWith your review
AcceptAccepts the lines and leaves a note on themThe accept at once; the note with your review
AskA question to the agentAt once; into the chat whenever the chat is offered
  • A flag shows a Flag badge, is listed under Flags in the Overview tab’s Ready to merge?, and marks its lines with a flag icon. Once it has gone out, its author resolves it with Resolve and accept these lines (or Resolve where its lines are not on screen), or reopens it with Reopen.
  • An accept shows an Accepted badge, an ask a Question badge.
  • Whenever the pull request offers the chat, Ask sends the question there, with the lines as context, and leaves no comment on the lines. See Agent chat.

Drafts and your review

Section titled “Drafts and your review”

Your comments, flags and accepts are drafts: only you see them, and each reads “goes out with your review”. A draft has Send now, to send it alone, and Delete. The Review button at the top right of every tab counts your pending drafts, and sending your review sends them all at once with a verdict and a note. The steps are in Accepting changes.

A script working as you can send a review with a personal API key; see Reviews.
